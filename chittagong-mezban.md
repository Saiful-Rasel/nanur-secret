# Chittagong Mezban

## Ingredients
- Beef
- Onion
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Red chilli powder
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Cooking oil

## Instructions
1. Prepare the beef and spices.
2. Cook the onion and spices.
3. Add beef and cook until tender.
4. Serve hot with rice.

## Serving Notes
- Serve hot with rice.
- Prepare enough for family and guests.
