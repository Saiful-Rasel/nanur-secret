# Nanu's Eid Biryani

## Ingredients
- Basmati rice
- Chicken
- Onion
- Yogurt
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Cardamom
- Cinnamon
- Salt
- Cooking oil

## Instructions
1. Wash and soak the rice.
2. Cook the chicken with spices, onion, and yogurt.
3. Cook the rice until nearly done.
4. Layer the rice and chicken.
5. Cook on low heat until done.
